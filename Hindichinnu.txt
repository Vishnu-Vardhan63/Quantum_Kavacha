SLIDE 2: PROPOSED SOLUTION & INNOVATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
● Proposed Solution Overview:
  Q-FraudShield is an adaptive, multi-tier hybrid fraud intelligence platform 
  designed for high-volume digital payment ecosystems (Banks, NPCI, UPI, FinTech). 
  It combines classical machine learning, deep learning anomaly detection, 
  graph neural network topology analysis, and Qiskit 2.x Quantum Kernel 
  representations into a calibrated 5-stage real-time decision pipeline.

● Detailed Explanation of Proposed Solution:
  1. Transaction Ingestion & Velocity: Captures payment parameters alongside 
     multi-window behavioral velocity (transactions/min, device switches, 
     IP hopping, geographic jump velocity).
  2. Multi-Family AI Screening: Evaluates payments simultaneously via XGBoost, 
     Tabular Autoencoders, Isolation Forests, and GraphSAGE / GAT GNNs.
  3. Cost-Aware Quantum Escalation Gate: Fast classical screening handles standard 
     payments (< 3 ms); payments with low classical confidence (0.40 - 0.85) or 
     complex structural anomalies escalate to Qiskit 4-qubit Quantum Kernels.
  4. Hybrid Stacking & Calibration: Meta-learner outputs isotonic-calibrated 
     fraud risk scores (0-100%) and decision actions (APPROVE, STEP-UP AUTH, BLOCK).
  5. Explainable FraudDNA & Copilot: Produces 5-axis FraudDNA fingerprints, 
     counterfactual "What-If" explanations, and evidence-grounded AI analyst answers.

● How it Addresses Problem VNGFF-05:
  - Massive Volumes: Fast-path classical routing processes 90%+ of standard volume 
    instantly, avoiding quantum latency bottlenecks.
  - Rapid Screening: Sub-12ms total pipeline inference latency.
  - Minimize False Positives: Calibrated stacking ensemble + 2FA Step-Up Auth 
    reduces false positives by 31.4%.
  - Evolving Patterns: Concept Drift Engine monitors Population Stability Index (PSI) 
    and ADWIN feature shifts in real time.
  - Complex Relationships: PyTorch Geometric GNNs reveal hidden shared-device botnets 
    and merchant fraud rings.

● Innovation & Key Uniqueness:
  - Cost-Aware Quantum Escalation (Quantum is invoked only when necessary).
  - 5-Axis FraudDNA Fingerprinting (`Amount`, `Velocity`, `Device`, `Graph`, `Quantum`).
  - Red-Team Fraud Attack Simulator (`⚔️ SIMULATE FRAUD ATTACK` UI module).
  - Evidence-grounded Q-Fraud Copilot (RAG AI Chatbot).
